# Med-Chain-ai
A blockchain-backed, AI-enhanced medical record system for India where every patient carries a single, tamper-proof health history accessible by any verified doctor or hospital with users permission. 

The AI of MED CHAIN scans the reports you assign. And analysis your medical reports within few seconds. Highlights and summarises important points. Gives the doctor a small description about your disease or illness. Restricts other users to access your reports. Your personal details are end-to-end- encrypted. Fully safe and secure data and reports. Send your reports directly to the doctors on MED CHAIN.
